## 1. Regulatory Context & Frameworks

### NCERT (National Council of Educational Research and Training)
- **Framework**: National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE 2023) and Foundational Stage (NCF-FS 2022) under National Education Policy (NEP 2020).
- **Curriculum Transition Status**:
  - **Classes 1 & 2**: New textbooks *Joyful Mathematics* (आनंदमय गणित) released under NCF-FS.
  - **Class 3**: New textbook *Maths Mela* (गणित मेला) introduced under NCF-SE 2023 (14 chapters).
  - **Classes 4 & 5**: *Math-Magic* (गणित का जादू) Rationalised Editions (transitioning to updated NCF series).
  - **Class 6**: New textbook *Ganita Prakash* (गणित प्रकाश) introduced under NCF-SE 2023 (10 chapters).
  - **Classes 7 & 8**: *Mathematics* (गणित) Rationalised Editions (13 chapters each).
  - **Classes 9 & 10**: *Mathematics* (गणित) Rationalised Editions (Class 9: 12 chapters; Class 10: 14 chapters).
- **Official Portal**: [https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php)

### CISCE / ICSE (Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations)
- **Framework**: Regulations and Syllabuses for Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE) Examination.
- **Classes 9 & 10**: Prescribed official ICSE Mathematics syllabus published directly by CISCE ([https://cisce.org](https://cisce.org)).
- **Classes 1 to 8**: CISCE provides the *Curriculum for Preschool to Class VIII (Mathematics)*; individual schools select CISCE-aligned textbooks (e.g. *Concise Mathematics* by Selina Publishers, *Understanding ICSE Mathematics* by M.L. Aggarwal). All content for Classes 1 to 8 in ShikshaGap is mapped strictly to the CISCE Curriculum Framework and marked as **"CISCE-aligned syllabus structure"**.

## 3. Strict Board Provenance Rules

1. **Board Isolation**:
   - Selecting **NCERT (CBSE)** draws exclusively from NCERT / CBSE tagged competencies, chapters, and question items.
   - Selecting **ICSE (CISCE)** draws exclusively from ICSE / CISCE tagged competencies, chapters, and question items.
   - Cross-board topics (e.g., Quadratic Equations or Fractions) are explicitly tagged with both `["CBSE", "ICSE"]` and carry board-specific pedagogical nuances (ICSE emphasizes explicit units, formal proofs, and multi-step expansion; NCERT emphasizes conceptual intuition and real-life modeling).
2. **Zero Verbatim Copyright Infringement**:
   - Textbook exercise questions and proprietary textbook passages are never copied verbatim.
   - All questions are **ORIGINALLY GENERATED** based strictly on the syllabus learning outcomes and verified through server-side deterministic computational checks.
3. **Unverified Items**:
   - There are currently **no unverified classes or boards** in the core Mathematics spectrum (Classes 1–10).
   - SCERT State Boards (Telangana and Andhra Pradesh) remain in architectural preview and are not served in scored assessments until full textbook digitisation.
